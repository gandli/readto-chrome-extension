小学故事

Once upon a time there was a little girl who lived with her mother in a small house near the forest. Every day the girl would walk through the forest to visit her grandmother who lived on the other side. One morning her mother said, "Here is some bread and butter for your grandmother. Take it to her, but do not talk to strangers on the way." The girl nodded and set off on the path through the trees. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. She was very happy.
